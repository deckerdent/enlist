# arc42 Architecture Description

This directory follows the arc42 section order. Sections are initial placeholders until implementation and stakeholder facts are documented.

1. [Introduction and Goals](./01-introduction-and-goals.md) — purpose, scope, requirements, quality goals, and stakeholders.
2. [Architecture Constraints](./02-architecture-constraints.md) — binding technical, organizational, regulatory, and convention constraints.
3. [Context and Scope](./03-context-and-scope.md) — system boundaries, actors, external systems, interfaces, and information flows.
4. [Solution Strategy](./04-solution-strategy.md) — architectural decisions and how they support quality goals.
5. [Building Block View](./05-building-block-view.md) — static decomposition, responsibilities, and relationships.
6. [Runtime View](./06-runtime-view.md) — important interactions, scenarios, and error paths.
7. [Deployment View](./07-deployment-view.md) — infrastructure, environments, component placement, and communication.
8. [Crosscutting Concepts](./08-crosscutting-concepts.md) — concepts such as security, persistence, errors, and observability.
9. [Architecture Decisions](./09-architecture-decisions.md) — links to decision records and their architectural relevance.
10. [Quality Requirements](./10-quality-requirements.md) — verifiable quality scenarios and acceptance criteria.
11. [Risks and Technical Debt](./11-risks-and-technical-debt.md) — material risks and debt, impact, next action, and status.
12. [Glossary](./12-glossary.md) — consistent definitions for domain terms and acronyms.

The [architecture-decisions](./architecture-decisions/index.md) directory contains individual decision records.
